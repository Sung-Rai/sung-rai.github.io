Fake News

Detecting fake news using ML and NLP methods

An Artificial Intelligence project that uses annotated data to train several different models to detect fake news. The Shallow Neural Network MLP model ended up being the most accurate with an accuracy of 0.9229 and an F1 score of 0.9086. It included feature engineering, as well as hyperparameter tuning to improve the model's performance, and Latent Dirichlet Allocation for topic discovery.

F# Interpreter

An Interpreter built in C# and F# for mathematics and graph plotting

An interpreter built to parse and evaluate mathematical expressions, as well as plot graphs of functions. It was built in F# with no mutables, and uses a C# GUI. The interpreter considers precedence rules, and was designed iteratively from the ground up, including the lexer, top-down recursive parser, and the Backus-Naur Form grammar.
