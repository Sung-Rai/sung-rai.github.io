AI Train Assistant

An Advanced AI chatbot written in Python

A deliverable for the Advanced Artificial Intelligence Module at the University of East Anglia. It uses Python, spaCy, experta, Keras, SQLite, and the official National Rail OJP API. Due to the requirements of the project, the repository is private.

A problem solving final year project achieving a first class presentation grade.

Balatro Four-Color Deck

When you need to differentiate the four suits.
